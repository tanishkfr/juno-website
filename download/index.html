Checking published installers…

Make yourself at home.

  1. Get the official installer.

    Download from the published GitHub release. Check its version and SHA-256 before opening it.

  2. Install with care.

    The beta installer is unsigned. Windows may show Unknown Publisher or SmartScreen. Verify its source and checksum; do not disable security protections. Managed PCs may require your administrator’s approval.

  3. Connect your provider.

    Use a supported account sign-in or API key in Juno. External providers need network access and their own quota; provider charges may apply.

Updates are manual. Review the release notes, keep a backup of your work, and install the next published version when you’re ready.
