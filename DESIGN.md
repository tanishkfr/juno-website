The desktop product's neutral palette, real artwork, and character profiles are inherited from UI-overhaul source 86b0f418. This independent marketing surface scales the typography and spacing for the web. No desktop sources are edited.

Dark ink #202623, secondary #59625b, white ground, warm neutral #f3f3ef, pale Kyoto #f3e8df, Seoul #f2e7ef. Characters retain all six canonical hues.
