A little character.
A considered workspace.

Juno is a local-first AI workspace for Windows, made by Tanishk.

Work has more than one shape.

Sometimes it starts with a question. Sometimes it needs a project, a plan, and a place to return to. Juno brings those moments into one desktop workspace.

Six companions give that workspace a little personality. Their starting characters are different; you choose the instructions, providers and tools they work with.

Familiar company. Clear boundaries.

Personality should make a tool easier to work with. It should also be clear what the tool can access, when it needs your approval, and what actually happened.

Local-first is a storage choice, not a claim that every model runs offline. Connected providers process the context you send them. Read the practical privacy boundaries.

Made by Tanishk.
